How CampusFix works

Follow a clear process from the first report to a completed repair.

  1. 1. Report the issue

    Describe the problem and where it happened. Include the details campus staff need to review it.

  2. 2. Review and assign

    An administrator reviews the report, sets its priority, and assigns it to a maintenance worker.

  3. 3. Track the repair

    Follow progress updates and confirm the repair when the work is complete.
